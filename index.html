Wallboard

Wallboard is a private, self-hosted wall calendar used by one person on a home network.

Privacy policy

Wallboard requests read-only access to Google Calendar to display events on a home display. Calendar data is stored only on the owner's private home server, is never shared with or sold to third parties, and is not used for any purpose other than displaying the calendar. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
